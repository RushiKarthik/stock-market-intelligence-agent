## 6. What I Should Learn Today

1. **Grey Market Premium (GMP):** An informal, unofficial premium at which an IPO stock is traded in an over-the-counter market before its official exchange listing. It reflects market sentiment but carries no legal guarantee or stock exchange backing.
2. **India VIX (Volatility Index):** A real-time index that measures the market's expectation of 30-day volatility based on Nifty option prices. A rising VIX suggests heightened market uncertainty or anticipated wider price fluctuations.
3. **IPO Registrar:** A SEBI-registered entity independent of the issuing company that processes application forms, manages the allotment process, and handles refund or share transfer instructions during an IPO (e.g., KFin Technologies).
